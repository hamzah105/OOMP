# Community code of conduct

Keep discussion respectful, specific and relevant to the project. Welcome people
with different backgrounds and experience. Critique technical ideas and evidence;
do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information or repeatedly
disrupt others. Treat research disagreements as opportunities to inspect methods
and results, including negative findings.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive material, close discussions or restrict
participation as appropriate. Decisions should protect participants and follow
GitHub's platform rules. No separate enforcement team or response SLA is implied.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through an available repository
channel; a public issue can request a confidential contact without identifying
people or publishing sensitive details. No dedicated private conduct-reporting
address is currently established in this repository. Do not post personal data
while requesting help. Vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
